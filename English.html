Pablo, 35-year-old pianist, he combines his teaching work as a professor of Piano Accompaniment for Instruments at the Superior Conservatory of Music of Seville with his performance career.

He has collaborated with orchestras such as the Cordats Symfonie Orkest of Rotterdam, Orquesta Promúsica, Orquesta y Coro New Filarmónica, and recently with the Málaga Philharmonic Orchestra, where he is part of the musician pool for Piano, Celesta, and Organ, under the direction of conductors such as Manuel Bustos, Elisa Gómez, Kristian Kalsen, and Susanna Mälkki.

Throughout his career, his trajectory has been recognized with 24 awards in national and international competitions, including first prizes at the Málaga Crea Young Performers Showcase (2019), the Río Órbigo National Competition (2008), the 1st Visitación Magarzo National Chamber Music Competition (2008), the 26th Marisa Montiel National Competition (2006), and the 10th Ángeles Reina National Competition (2000). He was also a finalist at the Rome Chamber Music Competition (2019).

He has performed on national and international stages across Germany, Spain, the Netherlands, England, and Portugal.

Since 2019, he has been a member of the Association of Composers and Performers of Málaga (ACIM), regularly participating in festivals dedicated to contemporary music. He has performed works by contemporary composers such as Zulema de la Cruz, Consuelo Díez, Javier Torres Maldonado, Tomás Marco, and Juan Luis Montoro.

In the realm of chamber music, he is a member of Masan Dúo (clarinet and piano) and Trío Destenay (clarinet, oboe, and piano), ensembles with which he regularly performs in national festivals and concert series.

His professional profile is rounded out by his experience as a harpsichordist and continuo player, collaborating with early music and period-instrument ensembles and orchestras such as L’Accademico Formato, the first professional resident baroque orchestra in Málaga.
