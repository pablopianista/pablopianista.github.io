A 35-year-old pianist, he combines his teaching work as a professor of Instrumental Accompaniment and Repertoire at the Conservatorio Superior de Música de Sevilla with his active career as a concert performer.

He has collaborated with orchestras such as the Cordats Symfonie Orkest of Rotterdam, Orquesta Promúsica, Orquesta y Coro New Filarmónica, and recently with the Málaga Philharmonic Orchestra (OFM), where he is part of the Piano, Celesta, and Organ ensemble pool, under the direction of conductors such as Manuel Bustos, Elisa Gómez, Kristian Karlsen, and Susanna Mälkki.

Throughout his career, his trajectory has been recognized with 24 awards in national and international competitions, notably including first prizes at: Málaga Crea-Muestra Jóvenes Intérpretes (2019), Concurso Nacional Río Órbigo (2008), I Certamen Nacional de Música de Cámara Visitación Magarzo (2008), XXVI Certamen Nacional Marisa Montiel (2006), and X Certamen Nacional Ángeles Reina (2000). He was also a finalist at the Rome International Chamber Music Competition (2019).

He has performed on national and international stages across Germany, France, Spain, the Netherlands, England, and Portugal.

Since 2019, he has been a member of the Association of Composers and Performers of Málaga (ACIM), regularly participating in festivals dedicated to contemporary music. He has performed works by contemporary composers such as Zulema de la Cruz, Consuelo Díez, Javier Torres Maldonado, Tomás Marco, and Juan Luis Montoro.

In the field of chamber music, he is a member of Masan Dúo (clarinet and piano) and Trío Destenay (clarinet, oboe, and piano), ensembles with which he performs regularly in national festivals and concert series.

His professional profile is complemented by his experience as a harpsichordist and continuo player alongside early music ensembles and historical instrument orchestras, such as L'Accademico Formato, the first professional resident baroque orchestra in Málaga.
